Who it is for

For teams about to spend real money.

  • Civic and public sectorPrograms that must show evidence of need to funders and oversight.
  • Mid-market companiesTeams that need senior research without a permanent hire.
  • Integrators and studiosPartners who want research depth behind their own client work.
  • Collections and cultural groupsOrganizations choosing tools to run what they hold, including with KURA.

A strong fit

  • You are about to fund a build or a rebuild and want evidence first.
  • Several groups must agree, such as leadership, funders and users.
  • You are mid-build and suspect the backlog is wrong.
  • You want senior research depth without hiring for it.

Probably not yet

  • Nobody with authority can act on what the research finds.
  • The idea is small enough to build and test in a few weeks.
  • The decision is made and no result would change it.

Questions

The objections, answered.

We already know our users.

Then the sprint confirms it quickly and gives you evidence to show funders and stakeholders. If something has changed, you learn that before the build and not after it.

We work in sprints and learn as we ship.

Shipping teaches you after the money is spent. The research sprint sharpens the bets your delivery sprints make, and it can run alongside them.

We do not have 90 days.

The sprint runs in parallel with planning and procurement, and weekly readouts put evidence in front of you from the first month. Ask on the scoping call whether a narrower scope fits.

Why not hire a researcher?

A permanent hire takes months to find and onboard, and one role cannot run fieldwork, synthesis and strategy at once. The sprint gives you senior depth now, a defined result and a playbook, without a long commitment.

What do you need from us?

A sponsor with authority to act, access to users or a way to reach them, and one point of contact for the weekly readouts. We handle the plan, recruiting support, fieldwork, analysis and the memo.

What does it cost?

A fixed fee, set at scoping from the number of user groups, the methods and the amount of recruiting involved. You get the figure in writing before anything starts.
