What counts as a fix?

One part of your business set up to run without you, such as a missed-call text-back, quote follow-ups, invoice reminders, review requests or online booking. Custom software, website rebuilds and hardware are not included.

I already use a scheduling or invoicing app.

Good. We usually make what you already pay for work harder before adding anything.

Can't I set this up myself?

Some of it, yes. What we bring is knowing which fix is worth your money, setting it up right the first time, and checking at 30 days that it pays.
