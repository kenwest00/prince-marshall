Five parts. One record.

  1. What you hold

    Catalogue records structured to museum standards, with controlled vocabularies instead of free text.

  2. Where it is

    A movement log, floor plans and inventory audits, so any object’s location can be reconstructed for any past date.

  3. What condition it is in

    Structured condition reports, treatment workflows and environmental monitoring, with photos compared over time.

  4. Where it came from

    Ordered provenance chains that flag gaps, plus a due-diligence checklist recording who checked what, and when.

  5. What it is worth

    Valuations that are never overwritten, insurance schedules of values, and loan and exhibition tracking.

Straight answers.

How do I get started with KÜRA?

Request a walkthrough. We show you KÜRA with your own kind of collection in mind, then scope a Collection Survey: our team comes on site to photograph, catalogue and, if you want, tag every work.

Can KÜRA give us a catalogue of our collection?

Yes. That is what the Collection Survey produces: every work photographed and catalogued to museum standards, with its marks, provenance and history. You can browse and search it, share it with researchers and lenders, publish chosen works online and export it at any time.

What happens during a Collection Survey?

Our team comes to you. Every work is photographed front and back, measured, condition-checked, catalogued and mapped to where it lives, with optional RFID tags. Your works never leave your building. You get the full record in KÜRA and a short findings report for your board.

Do you appraise or restore works?

No. We document, which keeps us independent. Appraisals, conservation treatment and art transport are done by qualified specialists, and we coordinate them for you.

Who owns our data?

You do. Your data leaves as easily as it arrives: open exports, no lock-in. The images we make are yours too.
